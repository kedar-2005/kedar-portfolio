Supply Chain Diagnostic Analysis

Power BI, Excel, PowerPoint. A beauty and personal-care company with 100 SKUs across cosmetics, haircare and skincare.

$577.6Ktotal revenue
46,099units sold
2.28%average defect rate
5.8 daysaverage shipping time

Inspection status across 100 SKUs: 36 failed, 41 pending, 23 other

36 failed41 pending23 other

What I found

  • 54% of SKUs hold less stock than a typical order. Skincare brings 42% of revenue but is the most exposed to stock-outs.
  • Supplier 5 has the highest defect rate (2.67%). Supplier 4 has the highest unit cost ($62.7 against a $47 average).
  • Carrier B handled $250K of revenue with the fastest shipping and lowest defects. Route B costs $596 against $485 for Route A.

What I recommended

  • Raise safety stock for skincare and set reorder points per SKU.
  • Re-inspect the 41 pending SKUs and run corrective action with high-defect suppliers.
  • Shift volume to Carrier B, use sea freight for slow-moving items, and renegotiate with Supplier 4 and on Route B.

Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

Excel, Power BI, PowerPoint. 200 transactions across 10 Istanbul shopping malls, 2021 to 2023.

What I found

  • Women made more purchases (118 against 82) and more revenue (₺243,390 against ₺221,958), while spend per visit was similar across genders.
  • Shoppers aged 56 and above bring about 30% of revenue. The 46 to 55 group is the weakest segment.
  • Payment method does not depend on gender, age or category.

What I recommended

  • Launch a loyalty program for shoppers aged 56 and above.
  • Survey or promote to the 46 to 55 segment to find out why it lags.
  • Focus inventory and promotions on Clothing and Shoes, and test gender-based assortment in Clothing.
